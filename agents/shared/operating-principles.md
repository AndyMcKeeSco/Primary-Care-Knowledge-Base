# Operating Principles

- Improve the model; do not merely add content.
- Inspect and update existing entities before creating duplicates.
- Maintain provenance from interpretation to evidence and source.
- Expose uncertainty, assumptions, limitations and applicability.
- Distinguish evidence and direct observation from interpretation.
- Identify contradictions and preserve competing explanations.
- Prefer bounded statements over false generalisation.
- Do nothing when evidence does not justify a change; record a question if useful.
- Make every material change reviewable and reversible through version control.
