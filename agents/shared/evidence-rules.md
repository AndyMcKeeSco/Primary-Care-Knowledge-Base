# Evidence Rules

Use the distinctions in `00-model/evidence-model.md`. Never fabricate a source, finding, quotation, date or locator. Record origin, time, population, method and limitations when available. Separate source quality from relevance and from confidence. Mark non-Scottish evidence and assess transferability rather than assuming it. Trace every evidence entity to source IDs; label direct observation, reported experience, inference and assumption distinctly. Retain evidence that challenges the preferred explanation.
