# Scottish GP headcount and estimated WTE at 31 March 2022

## Finding

Public Health Scotland reported a Scotland GP headcount of **4,514** and an estimated **3,494 whole-time equivalents (WTE)** at 31 March 2022, equivalent to **0.77 WTE per headcount**. The headcount excludes Specialist Trainee/Performer Registrar GPs. In this report, one GP WTE is defined as eight weekly contracted GP sessions.

## Source context

`SRC-0020` is an official Public Health Scotland Experimental Statistics release about the general-practice workforce in Scotland. The publication distinguishes NPCCD-derived GP headcount from survey-based estimated GP WTE; it does not present either measure as workload, capacity, access or care quality.

## Population and geography

The finding covers GPs in Scottish general practice recorded in the National Primary Care Clinicians Database (NPCCD), excluding Specialist Trainee/Performer Registrar GPs. It is reported at Scotland level.

## Method

GP headcount came from the NPCCD. The GP Data Collection Tool was sent to 911 practices; 707 practices responded by the closing date, and 517 practices remained with usable survey-response data after incomplete returns, returns without individual staff data and poor-quality returns were excluded. Public Health Scotland scaled WTE from responding-practice survey records with relevant session data to the national NPCCD headcount. Missing 2022 session data for salaried and retainer GP cohorts were imputed using 2019 WTE-to-headcount ratios by designation, age group and sex before final scaling.

## Time period

The measures are as at **31 March 2022**. The report was published on 29 November 2022 and the downloaded report incorporates revisions dated 21 December 2022 for historical 2013–2017 values; those historical values are not part of this finding.

## Applicability to Scotland

Directly applicable to Scotland because both the national headcount and estimated WTE are Scotland-level measures. Applicability is limited to the publication's included GP population and definitions and should not be extended to GP trainees, out-of-hours services, workload, effective capacity, access or quality of care.

## Limitations

The WTE is an estimate rather than an exact census value. It is based on 517 usable practice responses out of more than 900 practices, scaled to national NPCCD headcount. Missing session data for salaried and retainer GPs required imputation from 2019 cohort ratios. The report is designated Experimental Statistics and states that interpretation limitations apply. Comparisons with pre-2019 survey results require caution because the collection method changed in 2019, especially at NHS Board level. The headcount and WTE measures alone do not establish workload, capacity, access, implementation or care quality.
