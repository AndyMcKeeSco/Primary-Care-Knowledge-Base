# Most surveyed people rated their Scottish general-practice care positively in 2023/24, while 13% rated it poorly

## Finding

In the 2023/24 Scottish Health and Care Experience Survey, the weighted national responses to the question “Overall, how would you rate the care provided by your General Practice?” were 68.5% excellent or good, 18.6% fair and 12.9% poor or very poor. The national report presents these as 69%, 19% and 13% after rounding. It describes the positive rating as higher than in 2021/22 (66.5%, reported as 67%) but still below 2019/20 (79%).

These are respondents' reported perceptions of overall care. They do not establish the objective quality, safety, accessibility or outcomes of general-practice care.

## Source context

`SRC-0022` is the Scottish Government's national report for the 2023/24 Health and Care Experience Survey, published on 28 May 2024 as accredited official statistics. The finding uses the report, its supplementary chart data and its linked technical report as components of the same publication package.

## Population and geography

People aged 17 or over who lived in Scotland, were registered with a Scottish general practice on 25 September 2023 and responded to the survey. Public Health Scotland selected a practice-stratified random sample. Practices run by NHS Boards for particular groups, including people experiencing homelessness, armed-forces personnel and prisoners, were excluded. The result is a weighted Scotland-level estimate.

## Method

The survey invited 526,758 eligible sampled people and received 107,538 completed responses, an overall response rate of 20%. Initial invitations asked for online completion; a reminder included a paper questionnaire. National results were weighted for unequal selection probability by practice and for non-response by age and sex. The exact number of valid responses to the overall-care question was not reported in the report text or Figure 6 data inspected and may be lower than the total response count.

## Time period

Sampling used registration information from 25 September 2023. Fieldwork began on 23 October 2023, with reminders sent from 8 to 17 November 2023. Questions asked about experiences during the previous 12 months. The publication was released on 28 May 2024.

## Applicability to Scotland

Directly applicable to the weighted national experience reported by survey respondents registered with included Scottish general practices. It does not cover people not registered with a practice, excluded specialist practices, staff perceptions or community-pharmacy experience.

## Limitations

The 20% response rate creates a material risk of residual non-response bias despite weighting. Response rates varied substantially by age, deprivation and urbanity: they were 6% among people aged 17–24, 13% in the most deprived SIMD quintile and 16% in large urban areas, compared with 42% among people aged 65 or over, 26% in the least deprived quintile and 31% in remote rural areas. National weighting adjusted for age and sex, but not all of these dimensions directly. The source reports aggregated self-reported experience rather than direct observation; the broad overall-care item does not explain why respondents chose their ratings. The report notes that 2021/22 fieldwork occurred during the COVID-19 pandemic, which should be considered in comparisons. Rounded published percentages sum to 101%.
