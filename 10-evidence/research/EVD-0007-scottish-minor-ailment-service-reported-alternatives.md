# Most surveyed Scottish minor-ailment-service users reported general practice as their alternative

## Finding

Among 1,121 respondents using Scotland's community-pharmacy Minor Ailment Service in June–July 2018, 748 (67.1%) selected convenient location as their main reason for using the service. When asked what they would have done if the service were unavailable, 655 (59%) reported that they would have accessed general practice for treatment. At one-week follow-up, 281 of 327 respondents (over 85%) reported no further healthcare access for that minor ailment.

## Source context

`SRC-0034` is a cross-sectional national survey of people already using the Minor Ailment Service, followed by an optional one-week questionnaire. It measures reported preferences and subsequent self-report, not observed counterfactual diversion from general practice.

## Population and geography

Eligible service users attending participating Scottish community pharmacies during June and July 2018. The follow-up finding concerns the smaller self-selected group who returned the second questionnaire.

## Method

Community-pharmacy users received a pre-piloted questionnaire covering reasons for service choice, hypothetical alternative services, consultation experience and satisfaction. Consenting respondents could complete an optional follow-up about effectiveness and further healthcare use after seven days.

## Time period

June–July 2018, with one-week follow-up.

## Applicability to Scotland

Directly applicable to surveyed users of the 2018 Scottish Minor Ailment Service. The service preceded NHS Pharmacy First Scotland, so the results should not be treated as current programme activity or demand composition.

## Limitations

The sample included service users rather than the general population, and response and follow-up selection bias are possible. The general-practice alternative is hypothetical and does not demonstrate an avoided consultation. The study does not provide presenting-condition volumes in this bounded extraction. Current service eligibility, scope and behaviour may differ.
