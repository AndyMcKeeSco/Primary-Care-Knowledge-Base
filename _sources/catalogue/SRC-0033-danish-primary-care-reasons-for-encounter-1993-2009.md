# Reasons for encounter and disease patterns in Danish primary care: changes over 16 years

## Artefact

Cross-sectional registration study comparing reasons for encounter, diagnoses, contact types, follow-up and referral in Danish general practice in 2008–2009 with a similar 1993 survey.

## Source type

Peer-reviewed cross-sectional general-practice contact study with historical comparison.

## Author / publisher

Grete Moth, Frede Olesen and Peter Vedsted; *Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care*.

## Publication date

2012-06-01.

## Geographic origin

Central Denmark Region, Denmark.

## Locator

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.3109/02813432.2012.679230
- Open full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3378007/

## Access date

2026-09-02. Full article, methods and contact-distribution tables were inspected through PubMed Central.

## Rights / sensitivity

Open-access article reporting aggregate survey data. No record-level data are stored here.

## Quality notes

Directly relevant to reasons for encounter and follow-up in a GP-gatekeeping system. The study sampled one allocated day per participating GP across a year; 404 of 871 invited GPs participated. Staff contacts were excluded from reason-for-encounter distributions. Denmark's listing and gatekeeping model is comparable in some respects to Scotland, but financing, staffing, coding, period and service organisation differ.
