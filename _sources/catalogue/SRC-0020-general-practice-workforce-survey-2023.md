# General Practice Workforce Survey 2022

## Artefact

Public Health Scotland statistical publication about the Scottish general-practice workforce survey for 2022.

## Source type

Official statistics and survey publication.

## Author / publisher

NHS Education for Scotland (NES), published via the Turas Data Intelligence platform. Publication responsibility for the 2023 wave transferred from Public Health Scotland (PHS) to NES; earlier waves (up to and including 2022) remain PHS-hosted.

## Publication date

12 December 2023. Reference (census) date: 31 March 2023.

## Geographic origin

Scotland; Scottish national coverage.

## Locator

Authoritative publication landing page (NES Turas Data Intelligence): <https://turasdata.nes.nhs.scot/data-and-reports/official-workforce-statistics/all-official-statistics-publications/general-practice-workforce-survey-2023/>.

Full report: <https://turasdata.nes.nhs.scot/media/v00by43i/2023_gp_workforce_survey_report.html>.

Note: the previously recorded PHS locator (`.../general-practice-workforce-survey/general-practice-workforce-survey-2023/`) does not resolve to the 2023 release — PHS hosts the series only up to 2022, so that URL redirects to the 2022 publication. Do not use the PHS locator for 2023.

## Access date

2026-09-01. NES Turas landing page and full report locator verified as the correct 2023 publication package; downloadable tables and technical appendices not yet inspected.

## Rights / sensitivity

Public statistical publication. This catalogue record contains no row-level, patient-identifiable, or staff-identifiable data. Reuse terms were not rechecked.

## Quality notes

Likely a principal source for measured workforce composition and capacity in Scottish general practice. Survey response, weighting, missingness, definitions, headcount versus whole-time-equivalent measures, and comparability with earlier waves must be inspected before extracting findings. Publication authority does not remove survey non-response or measurement limitations. The 2023 wave changed publisher and platform (PHS → NES Turas); comparisons with PHS-published earlier waves must confirm consistent definitions and coverage rather than assuming continuity.

## Related evidence

None yet. Recommended for separate extraction of bounded workforce findings with denominators, dates, definitions, and limitations preserved.
