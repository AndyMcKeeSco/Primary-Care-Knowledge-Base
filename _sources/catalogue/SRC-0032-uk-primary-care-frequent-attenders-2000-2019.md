# Consultation patterns and frequent attenders in UK primary care from 2000 to 2019

## Artefact

Retrospective cohort analysis of 1,699,709,314 consultation events from 12,330,545 patients in 845 UK general practices using CPRD GOLD data from April 2000 to March 2019.

## Source type

Peer-reviewed analysis of routinely collected primary-care electronic records.

## Author / publisher

Evangelos Kontopantelis, Maria Panagioti, Tracey Farragher, Luke A Munford, Rosa Parisi, Claire Planner, Sharon Spooner, Alice Tse, Darren M Ashcroft and Aneez Esmail; *BMJ Open*.

## Publication date

2021-12-20.

## Geographic origin

United Kingdom; aggregate findings include England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland without separable national estimates for the main measures.

## Locator

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-054666
- Open full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8718478/

## Access date

2026-09-02. Complete article XML, tables and limitations were inspected through PubMed Central.

## Rights / sensitivity

Open-access research article. It reports anonymised aggregate data; no patient-level data are stored here.

## Quality notes

Large longitudinal UK dataset with sensitivity analyses using several frequent-attender thresholds. CPRD GOLD was representative by age, sex and deprivation but only broadly geographically representative because Vision-system use was clustered; Scottish practices were more heavily represented in later years. Exact UK estimates must not be treated as Scottish estimates.
