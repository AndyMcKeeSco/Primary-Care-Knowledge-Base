## Quality notes

Directly relevant, large-scale Scottish empirical research for the distribution of multimorbidity and its association with deprivation. Its strength is measured activity across many practices rather than reported workflow experience. Cross-sectional routine-data analysis cannot by itself establish causality or explain how staff and patients experience care.
