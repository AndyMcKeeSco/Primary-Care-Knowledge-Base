# Generative artificial intelligence in primary care: an online survey of UK general practitioners

## Artefact

Peer-reviewed online survey of UK general practitioners concerning reported use of generative-AI tools in clinical practice.

## Source type

Cross-sectional clinician survey.

## Author / publisher

Charlotte R. Blease, Cosima Locher, Jens Gaab, Maria Hägglund and Kenneth D. Mandl; *BMJ Health & Care Informatics*.

## Publication date

2024-09-17.

## Geographic origin

United Kingdom; UK-wide results are not presented as separable Scottish estimates.

## Locator

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjhci-2024-101102
- Open full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11429366/

## Access date

2026-09-03. Full article, questionnaire description, results table and limitations were inspected through PubMed Central.

## Rights / sensitivity

Open-access research article reporting anonymised aggregate survey results.

## Quality notes

Directly relevant to reported UK GP adoption and task use of general-purpose generative AI. The Doctors.net.uk sample was non-probability and may be affected by participation and enthusiasm bias. Self-reported use does not establish safety, effectiveness, policy compliance or routine NHS adoption; pooled UK results must not be treated as Scottish estimates.
