# CARE Plus multimorbidity trial in deprived areas

## Artefact

Peer-reviewed article titled *Effectiveness of a primary care-based complex intervention to promote self-management in patients with multimorbidity in deprived areas (CARE Plus): a pragmatic cluster randomised controlled trial*. Cataloguing the trial does not establish that the intervention is effective or transferable.

## Source type

Peer-reviewed pragmatic cluster randomised controlled trial with a complex-intervention process context.

## Author / publisher

Stewart W. Mercer and colleagues; published by *BMC Medicine*.

## Publication date

2016.

## Geographic origin

Scotland. The intervention was studied in general practices serving areas of high socioeconomic deprivation; it should not be generalised beyond the recruited settings and population without assessment.

## Quality notes

Direct Scottish field research about a defined primary-care intervention in deprived settings. The pragmatic design is valuable for implementation-focused questions, while cluster allocation, recruitment, follow-up, intervention fidelity and context may limit interpretation and transferability. Outcomes and reported experiences should be extracted separately, and neither should be converted directly into claims about routine Scottish primary care.
