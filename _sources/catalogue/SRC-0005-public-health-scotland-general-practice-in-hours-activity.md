## Quality notes

Official Scottish source candidate for measured, recorded in-hours general-practice activity. The January 2026 release is labelled "Official statistics in development" and covers data recorded from January 2018 through January 2026. It reports approximately 93% practice coverage, but not all activity is recorded and encounter complexity or duration is unavailable. Encounters are not synonymous with appointments, because they can include indirect work without patient contact.

The January 2026 release excludes eight practices affected by clinical-system migration data-quality issues and the publication was subsequently paused while those issues were investigated. Recording changes, default encounter entries, variable working days, unmapped staff groups and revisions limit trend and category comparisons. The data are not directly comparable with NHS England or NHS Wales activity publications because counting and classification methods differ.

The artefact was selected as a high-authority source candidate, not as evidence. Authority, relevance and accessibility remain separate considerations, and statistics must be extracted from a named release with its provenance and limitations preserved.
