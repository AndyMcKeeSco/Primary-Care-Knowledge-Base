# Primary care in Scotland

## Artefact

National performance-audit report examining the reform of primary care in Scotland. It is catalogued as a candidate source rather than as evidence or an endorsement of its findings.

## Source type

Performance audit using mixed documentary, quantitative and field-research inputs.

## Author / publisher

Audit Scotland.

## Publication date

2023.

## Geographic origin

Scotland. The report concerns national reform and draws on material from Scottish primary-care organisations and stakeholders; any local examples remain bounded to their stated contexts.

## Quality notes

High institutional authority for public audit and useful national coverage. Its combination of national data, document review and engagement with primary-care stakeholders makes it a strong starting point for field-research questions about implementation. Audit evidence describes the period and participants selected by the audit and should not be treated as an exhaustive account of every Board, partnership, practice or pharmacy. Intended reform, reported experience and measured activity must be separated during extraction. Full text was not inspected in this catalogue pass.
