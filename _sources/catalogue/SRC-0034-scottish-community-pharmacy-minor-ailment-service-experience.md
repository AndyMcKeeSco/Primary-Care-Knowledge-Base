# Public perceptions and experiences of the minor ailment service in community pharmacy in Scotland

## Artefact

National cross-sectional survey of people accessing Scotland's community-pharmacy Minor Ailment Service during June and July 2018, with an optional one-week follow-up.

## Source type

Peer-reviewed service-user survey and follow-up.

## Author / publisher

Lee Boag, Katie Maclure, Anne Boyter, Scott Cunningham, Gazala Akram, Harry McQuillan and Derek Stewart; *Pharmacy Practice*.

## Publication date

2021-02-12.

## Geographic origin

Scotland.

## Locator

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.18549/PharmPract.2021.1.2152
- Open full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7886314/

## Access date

2026-09-02. Complete article XML, methods and reported results were inspected through PubMed Central.

## Rights / sensitivity

Open-access article reporting aggregate survey responses. No respondent-level data are stored here.

## Quality notes

Direct Scottish evidence about use of a community-pharmacy service for self-limiting conditions and reported alternatives. Results are self-reported by service users, the alternative-service question is hypothetical, and follow-up response was incomplete. The 2018 Minor Ailment Service predates NHS Pharmacy First Scotland and should not be assumed to describe the current service.
