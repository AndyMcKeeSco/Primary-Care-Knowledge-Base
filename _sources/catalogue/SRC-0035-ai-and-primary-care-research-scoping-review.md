# Artificial Intelligence and Primary Care Research: A Scoping Review

## Artefact

Peer-reviewed scoping review mapping the nature and extent of international research involving artificial intelligence and primary care.

## Source type

Scoping review of published and grey literature.

## Author / publisher

Jacqueline K. Kueper, Amanda L. Terry, Merrick Zwarenstein and Daniel J. Lizotte; *The Annals of Family Medicine*.

## Publication date

2020-05-01.

## Geographic origin

Canada-based authorship with internationally sourced literature.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1370/afm.2518

## Access date

2026-09-03. Full article, methods, search strategy and reported limitations were inspected through PubMed Central.

## Rights / sensitivity

Open full-text scholarly article. No patient-level data are stored here.

## Quality notes

Useful baseline map of AI purposes, primary-care functions, users, conditions, geographies and methods. Searches covered 11 published or grey-literature databases and followed PRISMA-ScR. Its search predates the current generative-AI wave, so it should be paired with newer sources and not used as a current adoption estimate or evidence of effectiveness.
