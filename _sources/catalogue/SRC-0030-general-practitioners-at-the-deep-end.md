# General Practitioners at the Deep End

## Artefact

Report titled *General Practitioners at the Deep End: The experience and views of general practitioners working in the most severely deprived areas of Scotland*, published as Royal College of General Practitioners Occasional Paper 89. It records a bounded practitioner enquiry and is not evidence about all Scottish practices by itself.

## Source type

Qualitative practitioner field research and programme report.

## Author / publisher

Graham Watt; Royal College of General Practitioners, with the University of Glasgow Deep End project as the authoritative programme source.

## Publication date

2012.

## Geographic origin

Scotland, specifically general practices serving highly deprived populations. The purposive setting is central to relevance and limits generalisation to other Scottish primary-care contexts.

## Quality notes

Directly relevant to practitioner-reported experience in Scottish general practices serving highly deprived communities and valuable for generating bounded workflow and problem questions. It is not a representative survey of Scottish general practice, and participant selection, facilitation, analysis and historical context require examination in the full report. The programme's institutional commitment to the Deep End perspective should be recorded as an advocacy or institutional bias risk, not used to dismiss the accounts. Full text was not inspected in this catalogue pass.
