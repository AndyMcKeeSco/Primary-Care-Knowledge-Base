# Perceptions of Scottish primary care — balanced source discovery

## Question

Which verified, accessible sources describe what patients, carers and staff value or experience as poor, frustrating or failing in Scottish general practice and community pharmacy, with results that can later support bounded evidence extraction for `QUE-0001`?

## Population

- Patients and service users, including groups whose experience may be underrepresented in national surveys.
- Unpaid carers where sources report their perspective separately.
- Clinical, administrative and multidisciplinary primary-care staff.

Do not combine these populations silently. Preserve each source's sampling frame, response rate and exclusions.

## Settings

- Scottish general practice.
- Scottish community pharmacy, including NHS Pharmacy First Scotland and relevant predecessor services when clearly dated.
- Cross-setting primary-care experience where general-practice or community-pharmacy findings are separable.

## Geography

Prioritise Scotland-wide evidence, followed by clearly bounded NHS Board, Health and Social Care Partnership, cluster or practice-level Scottish studies. Use UK-wide or overseas sources only when no adequate Scottish source is found for a material gap; label jurisdiction and transferability explicitly and do not treat them as Scottish evidence.

## Period

Prioritise sources published from 2018 onward. Retain older sources only when they document an enduring experience theme, a predecessor service, a failed or discontinued initiative, or a necessary historical comparator. Search for current, revised, superseded and withdrawn versions.

## Intended use

Create a balanced, deduplicated set of verified source candidates for later Evidence Extractor assignments. Cover positive and negative experience rather than searching only for dissatisfaction. Distinguish overall satisfaction scores, specific reported experiences, qualitative accounts, staff perception and measured service outcomes.

Source discovery and catalogue records do not establish findings, validate claims or authorise service changes.

## Existing material to inspect and avoid duplicating

- `QUE-0001`.
- `SRC-0022` and draft `EVD-0008` for national general-practice overall-care ratings.
- `SRC-0034` and draft `EVD-0007` for service-user experience of the 2018 community-pharmacy Minor Ailment Service.
- Existing discovery reports and every record under `_sources/catalogue/`.

Existing material leaves staff perceptions, carers, reasons behind ratings, poorly experienced aspects, current community-pharmacy experience and variation between population groups insufficiently covered.

## Search routes

Search and document at least these applicable routes:

1. Scottish Government and Public Health Scotland surveys, reports, supplementary tables and technical reports.
2. Healthcare Improvement Scotland, Care Inspectorate, Audit Scotland and Scottish Parliament material.
3. NHS Board, HSCP and national programme evaluations.
4. Professional bodies, regulators, workforce surveys and representative organisations for staff experience.
5. Peer-reviewed qualitative, quantitative and mixed-methods research.
6. Citation trails, earlier/later versions, evaluations, limitations, failure, discontinuation and lessons learned.

Record exact queries or reproducible search descriptions, access dates, filters, screening boundary and stopping rationale.

## Required output

- Write one source-discovery report to `_sources/discovery/source-find-001-que-0001.md` using the Source Finder's required candidate fields.
- Retain no more than eight candidates and create no more than four new `SRC` records.
- Allocate permanent IDs only after repository-wide duplicate and ID checks.
- Update `_sources/catalogue.md` when a new source record is created.
- Append one outcome row to `_sources/source-finding-log.md`.
- Recommend bounded follow-on Evidence Extractor assignments, but do not create `EVD`, `OBS`, `CLAIM`, `PRB`, `HYP` or `OPP` records.

## Constraints

- Read and follow `AGENTS.md`, `agents/research/source-finder.md`, shared evidence, clinical-safety and repository-write rules, and the naming and relationship models.
- Use only public, non-sensitive material; do not collect or store patient-identifiable or confidential data.
- Verify complete artefact identity and access to the extent claimed. Search-result snippets are discovery aids only.
- Keep patient, carer and staff perspectives distinct; keep general practice and community pharmacy distinct.
- Preserve contradictory, negative, positive, failed, superseded and inaccessible material.
- Do not infer care quality, safety, causality or Scottish applicability from satisfaction, title, source authority or search rank.
- Stop at 45 minutes, eight retained candidates, four new source records or diminishing returns, whichever occurs first.
- If the task is blocked, write no partial production-looking source record; log the blocker and smallest safe next action.
