# Evidence Model

## Source
An external or internal artefact: policy document, research paper, dataset, interview transcript, evaluation, website, or practice observation notes. A source is not itself an endorsed finding.

## Evidence
A structured, bounded finding derived from one or more sources, with population, method, time, geography and limitations where known.

## Claim
A proposition that evidence can support, challenge or qualify.

## Observation
Something directly seen, recorded or reported during research. Reported experience must be labelled as such and is not automatically generalisable.

## Hypothesis
A proposition requiring testing.

## Assumption
An unsupported proposition used provisionally. Label it explicitly, state the consequence if wrong, and create a question where material.

```text
SOURCE → produces → EVIDENCE
EVIDENCE → supports/challenges → CLAIM
OBSERVATION → supports/challenges → CLAIM
CLAIM → may contribute to → HYPOTHESIS
EXPERIMENT → tests → HYPOTHESIS
```

Provenance should permit a reviewer to move from interpretation back to the artefact. Evidence records quote or paraphrase only what the source warrants and record applicability and contradictions.
