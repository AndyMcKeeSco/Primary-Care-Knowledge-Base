# Confidence Model

Allowed values are `unknown`, `low`, `medium`, and `high`.

Confidence is the model's current warranted belief in an entity's central proposition; it is not synonymous with the quality of any single source. Assess amount and quality of evidence, independence/replication, consistency, recency, geographical applicability, contradictory evidence, and degree of inference.

- **unknown:** not assessed or insufficient basis.
- **low:** tentative, sparse, indirect, inconsistent or poorly applicable support.
- **medium:** meaningful support with material limitations or uncertainty.
- **high:** strong, consistent, independently replicated and applicable support, with contradictions addressed.

State rationale when changing confidence. Agents must never upgrade confidence merely because several documents repeat the same unsupported claim. A high-quality source can challenge confidence; many dependent sources count as one lineage. Confidence may fall as well as rise.
