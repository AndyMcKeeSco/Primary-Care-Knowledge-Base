# Scope

## In scope
Scottish primary-care organisations, settings, people, journeys, work, decisions, boundaries, problems, evidence, constraints, interventions and learning. Initial depth is general practice and community pharmacy.

## Out of scope for V1
Clinical guidance, patient-level records, exhaustive research population, product implementation, automated clinical decisions, databases and agent orchestration. Evidence from elsewhere may be included only with origin and Scottish applicability made explicit.
