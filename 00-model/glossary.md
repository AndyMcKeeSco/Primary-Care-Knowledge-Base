# Glossary

- **Entity:** a typed Markdown record with permanent ID.
- **Edge:** a typed relationship to another entity ID.
- **Provenance:** traceable origin and transformation history.
- **Pattern:** a synthesis of repeated or varying observations, not automatically a general truth.
- **Applicability:** relevance of evidence to the population, geography and context in question.
- **Lifecycle:** explicit status transitions that preserve history.
- **Synthetic:** invented solely to demonstrate model structure and never evidence.
