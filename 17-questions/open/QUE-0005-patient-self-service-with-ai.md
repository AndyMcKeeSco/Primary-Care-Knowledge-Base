# What could patients self-serve with AI?

## Question

Which patient needs currently requiring a primary-care contact could patients safely and effectively meet themselves with AI assistance — for example information, navigation, triage support, administrative self-service or self-management?

## Why it matters

Safe self-service could reduce avoidable demand and improve access for some groups, while risking harm, exclusion or false reassurance for others. Distinguishing the two is essential before recommending any patient-facing AI, and directly serves the access and capacity objectives.

## Decision enabled

Which patient-facing self-service opportunities are worth prototyping, and which needs must remain human-mediated on safety, equity or trust grounds.

## Current knowledge

Not yet established. No evidence characterises current patient-initiated contacts by whether they could be self-served, nor the digital access and literacy constraints that would bound self-service.

## Evidence gap

No findings connect contact reasons to self-service feasibility, and none assess the equity risk of shifting effort onto patients.

## Proposed method

Extract evidence on demand composition and contact reasons (see `QUE-0010`); assess candidate needs against safety, digital-exclusion and health-literacy constraints; treat any triage or clinical-advice function as requiring qualified clinical and safety review before testing. No patient-level data or clinical advice generated here.

## Priority rationale

High strategic value and strong alignment with the AI focus of the objective, tempered by significant safety and equity dependencies.

## Dependencies

Depends on `QUE-0010` (demand composition) and `QUE-0009` (access inequalities); constrained by `QUE-0015` (guardrails). Relates to personas `PER-0001`–`PER-0009`.

## Answer/closure criteria

Closed when at least one patient need is evidenced as a safe, equitable self-service candidate — with its population, the AI role, and the safety and exclusion risks stated — and unsuitable needs are explicitly identified.
