# Which existing digital tools are underused in primary care, and what blocks adoption?

## Question

Which digital tools already available in Scottish primary care (for example video consulting, patient portals, e-prescribing, messaging) are underused relative to their potential, and what blocks their adoption?

## Why it matters

Before proposing new technology, the model should understand why existing tools succeed or fail. Adoption barriers (workflow fit, trust, training, infrastructure, incentives) usually recur, so learning them prevents repeating avoidable failures with AI.

## Decision enabled

Whether a proposed opportunity should build new capability or instead remove adoption barriers to existing tools, and which barriers any new tool must overcome.

## Current knowledge

Not yet established. No evidence characterises tool usage or adoption barriers in the repository.

## Evidence gap

No extracted findings describe usage levels or the reasons for under-adoption of existing digital tools.

## Proposed method

Extract evidence from evaluation, audit and implementation sources; classify barriers by type (workflow, trust, training, infrastructure, incentive, equity). Distinguish tool availability from effective use. Mark Scottish applicability.

## Priority rationale

Medium-to-high: a strong guard against reinventing failed adoption, and a source of reusable implementation constraints.

## Dependencies

Feeds constraints (`12-constraints`, currently empty) and relates to `QUE-0005`, `QUE-0015`.

## Answer/closure criteria

Closed when at least one existing tool's under-use is evidenced with its adoption barriers characterised and Scottish applicability stated.
