An intentionally broad placeholder for a non-GP professional or staff role contributing to care or support associated with Scottish general practice. It does not imply that all such actors are employed by, based in or available to every practice.

The broad grouping is temporary. `PER-0004` to `PER-0009` now expose requested reception, nursing, specialist-nursing, phlebotomy, practice-management and healthcare-support perspectives for research.
