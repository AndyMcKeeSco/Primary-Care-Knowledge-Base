# Scottish community pharmacy

## Definition

A setting archetype for care work occurring in Scottish community pharmacy. The catalogued national pharmaceutical-care strategy is a source for intended Scottish policy, while the Public Health Scotland community-pharmacy gateway and community-prescribing series identify possible sources of activity data. This record does not infer current operations or outcomes from policy intent or publication titles.

This is not a profile of an individual pharmacy, organisation or service.

## Scope

- **Setting category:** community pharmacy
- **Archetype:** Scottish community-pharmacy delivery environment
- **Geography:** Scotland
- **Included:** pharmaceutical-care policy intent and the research dimensions represented by the catalogued community-pharmacy and community-prescribing data sources.
- **Excluded:** individual premises, patient-level data, clinical guidance, and work occurring outside community pharmacy unless a later interface record explicitly connects it.

## Actors

No bounded workforce or actor finding has been extracted from the catalogued research. Actor roles, responsibilities and variation therefore remain unknown in this version of the setting.

## Services

The pharmaceutical-care strategy describes intended policy at its 2017 publication date, but it does not by itself establish current service configuration or implementation. The exact services covered by the Public Health Scotland data gateway may change with policy, coding and publication design and have not been verified release by release. Services are therefore not enumerated here.

## Typical work

The community-prescribing series is a candidate source for items dispensed and reimbursed in the community. Those measures do not by themselves establish prescribing intent, medicine consumption, adherence, clinical appropriateness or pharmacy workload. No typical-work sequence or frequency is asserted without a bounded evidence extraction.

## Variation

Variation by service, premises, geography, population and time has not been characterised from extracted evidence. The age of the strategy and possible subsequent service changes are material limits on using it to describe a current setting.

## Constraints

No setting-specific constraint has been established through an evidence record. Policy intent, gateway contents and dispensing measures must remain distinct: none is a substitute for verified operational, workforce, experience or outcome evidence.
